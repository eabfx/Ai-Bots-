Import from Notion

In Notion open your Trade Journey database, choose Export, format Markdown & CSV (include subpages), then put the downloaded .zip in the trader\import folder next to your keys. Press Preview to see what it would add. Nothing is saved until you press Import. Screenshots are copied to your PC with the trades, and trades already in the journal are skipped.

Import from TradeZella

Your TradeZella backtesting trades and demo-account trades, copied through the TradeZella connection into trader\tradezella. Backtest trades go to the Backtest page as a percent of the practice balance with their R. Demo trades are kept but not counted. Press Preview to see what it would add. Nothing is saved until you press Import, and trades already in the journal are skipped.

Import from a broker CSV

Choose the trade history file your broker or TradingView exports. It is read in this browser and nothing is uploaded. You check the columns and a preview before anything is added, and trades already in the journal are skipped.

DateTimeInstrumentDirP/L
Times are used exactly as they appear in the file, so they only count as Dublin time if your broker exports Dublin time. Many brokers put the opening side on a closing row, so check the direction column in the preview. If your profit column is already net of fees, leave fees empty.

Journal coach

An AI review of your journal and the Indicator Stats. Pressing the button sends your logged trades (date, instrument, direction, tag, risk, result, notes) to Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini), whichever key you saved. It reviews past trades. It never places an order and never tells you what to take.

This browser is blocking local storage, so trades only last for this session. Use Backup JSON before you close the tab.
